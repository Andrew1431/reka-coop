Tested and working

  • Joining over Steam invites
  • Seeing each other: movement, looks, names, gear
  • One shared backpack, pickups, chopping
  • Talks: whoever starts one leads it
  • Story choices, quest and unlock popups for all
  • Tutorial steps: anyone can advance them
  • Carrying and dropping objects
  • Weather, time of day, cinematics
  • Map highlights shared by everyone

Works, known issues

  • The hut's legs on guests' screens while it walks
  • Trees around the hut when it sits (guests)
  • A raised lantern's glow isn't shown to others
  • First talk right after travelling
  • Building: works, the building tutorial is skipped

Not tested yet

  • Everything after the prologue
  • Merchants: offers and buying
  • 3 and 4 players
  • Joining by IP over the internet
  • Guests driving the hut
