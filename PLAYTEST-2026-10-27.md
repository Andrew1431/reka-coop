# Playtest 2026-10-27: new campaign, tutorial and first area (two copies, one PC)

Host started a brand new game; guest joined after the character creator (`mod/play.ps1`).
Status of each issue is marked; see the end for what was fixed after the session.

## Decisions to make first
- **Inventory model: decided, everything shared** (items, money, building blocks and plans, clothes). One pack in the host's save; good for drop-in/drop-out.
- **Conversations: decided, whoever starts one pulls everyone in** (built in 0.3.8). The story runs once, on the host; the starter picks the options. Feedback was: the guest should be pulled into the host's conversations (same camera, no roaming), and the witch scene felt better than blocking.
- **Joining before the fog.** Only let guests join once the host has gone through the fog (see STATUS.md).
